V-Slender treatments available at Vivace

All weight management treatments at Vivace are medically supervised by Dr. Natasha, and designed around your body, your health and your goals.

Signature Doctor-guidedMetabolic supportMedical monitoringComprehensive blood work

Medically Supervised Weight Management

Physician-supervised weight management programs tailored to your unique health profile and body goals. Administered under clinical care at Vivace, our protocols combine doctor-guided appetite management, metabolic support, regular medical monitoring, and comprehensive blood work. ↗

Allurion Balloon

Swallowable, no surgery, 16 weeks A swallowable intragastric balloon that needs no endoscopy, no anaesthesia and no surgery. The capsule is swallowed with water and inflated in minutes, then occupies space in the stomach for 16 weeks, reducing portion sizes and supporting significant weight loss. →

Endomina

Incisionless stomach suturing A transoral endoscopic procedure that reduces the size of the stomach from the inside, without any incisions or cuts. Performed under sedation, it creates sutures that narrow stomach capacity, producing restriction similar to bariatric surgery with none of the surgical risk. →

Metabolic Lab Checks

The data behind your plan Comprehensive blood panels assessing thyroid function, blood sugar, insulin resistance, hormonal balance, lipid profile and nutritional deficiencies. They identify metabolic barriers to weight loss, guide your treatment plan, and are included in every V-Slender programme. →

Your V-Slender programme

Medically guided, monitored and personalised, your weight management journey at Vivace is structured for sustainable results.

  1. 01
    Medical consultation & assessment

    Our doctor reviews your health history, weight goals and previous attempts. We order baseline lab work to understand your metabolic profile before recommending a programme.

  2. 02
    Personalised programme selection

    Based on your labs, BMI and goals, we recommend the most appropriate treatment: a medically supervised weight management programme, the Allurion balloon, Endomina suturing or a combination. You receive a clear plan with timelines and expected milestones.

  3. 03
    Treatment & regular monitoring

    Regular check-ins, weight tracking and lab reviews throughout your programme. We adjust protocols as needed and ensure your health is monitored at every step.

  4. 04
    Maintenance & lifestyle support

    Once you reach your goal, we support the transition to long-term maintenance, combining medical protocols with lifestyle guidance to sustain your results.

Our medically supervised programmes are generally suited to adults with a BMI of 27 or above, particularly those with weight-related health conditions such as type 2 diabetes, hypertension or high cholesterol. Some options are not suitable during pregnancy or with certain medical histories, so our doctor will assess your suitability at a medical consultation.

Most people find it no harder than swallowing a large tablet. The capsule goes down with a glass of water, is filled through a thin tube once it is in your stomach, and the tube is then removed. Some nausea or cramping is common for the first few days while your stomach adjusts, and we give you medication and guidance to manage it.

It depends on your starting point, the treatment you choose and how closely you follow the programme. We set realistic, individual milestones at your consultation based on your labs and BMI, and track progress at every check-in, rather than promising a single figure that may not apply to you.

Endomina is performed through the mouth with an endoscope while you are sedated, so there are no incisions or scars. Sutures are placed inside the stomach to reduce its capacity. Most patients go home the same day and move gradually from liquids back to normal food over the following weeks, with our team supporting you throughout.
